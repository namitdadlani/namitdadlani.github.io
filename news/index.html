The ‘Exotic Physics’ Behind a Mysterious 1,000-Mile Cloud on Mars

Scientists have finally modeled how a mysterious 1,000-mile cloud forms over Mars's 65,000-foot volcano Arsia Mons each morning. According to a study in Nature Geoscience, the Arsia Mons Elongated Cloud forms through homogeneous nucleation, where water vapor turns directly into ice crystals without clinging to dust particles. This rare process requires a 30-degree Celsius temperature drop in 10 minutes and relative humidity levels 100,000 times higher than any recorded on Earth.

Why living alone is becoming increasingly unaffordable for Torontonians

Single-person households in Toronto face a growing financial squeeze as basic living costs outpace average earnings. Research by the Wellesley Institute shows a single adult under 45 needs $61,654 after tax annually to live comfortably, far above the city's median single income of $45,200. With monthly rent, groceries, and utilities averaging $2,783, single residents are heavily burdened. Consequently, single-person households now represent 44.1 percent of food bank users nationwide.
