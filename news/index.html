An Ontario nature sanctuary home to over 70 rare species will now be protected for generations to come

Conservation group Ontario Nature has officially acquired Thickson’s Woods, ensuring permanent protection for a rare 10-hectare sanctuary on Lake Ontario. Located near Whitby and Oshawa, the land features 150- to 250-year-old white pine trees and protects over 75 rare species. Local volunteers originally formed a land trust in 1983 to save the forest from commercial logging. After four decades of community stewardship, the aging volunteer board transferred ownership to preserve the site as Ontario Nature’s 27th protected reserve.
