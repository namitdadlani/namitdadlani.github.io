Fridges in French supermarkets hit by cyber attack

A cyber attack targeted refrigeration software across France, causing fridges and freezers in roughly 400 supermarkets to heat up and spoil millions of euros worth of fresh and frozen food. Hackers compromised Danfoss remote management software used by major chains like Intermarché and Carrefour, forcing units to warm up to 50 degrees Celsius overnight. Danfoss stated that the affected units were older models whose security software could not be updated.

Ontario seniors care experts call on province to fund 'missing middle' housing

A new policy report by Ontario seniors care experts calls on the province to fund Enhanced Assisted Living housing to keep older adults off long-term care waitlists. The report highlights successful models like WoodGreen's cluster care, which diverts 83 percent of residents from nursing homes at half the daily cost. Experts warn that Ontario's senior population is projected to grow to 4.6 million people over the next 25 years.
