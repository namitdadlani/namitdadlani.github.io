The New York Times sues Brave web browser for AI scraping and bypassing paywalls

The New York Times, along with the New York Daily News, Chicago Tribune, and Orlando Sentinel, is suing Brave Software for allegedly stealing news content. The lawsuits claim the Brave web browser lets users bypass paywalls and sells access to scraped publisher data to train AI models for companies like Anthropic and Perplexity. Brave never asked for permission or compensated publishers. Defending his firm, Perplexity's communications chief Jesse Dwyer countered that the Times overpays its attorneys and underpays its journalists.

Shop freezers in seven European countries hit by cyber-attack

A major cyberattack has disrupted commercial freezers and refrigerators across supermarkets in seven European countries, pushing temperatures inside the units up to 50 degrees Celsius. The attack targeted equipment evaporators, forcing them into defrost mode and ruining large quantities of food. Impacted businesses span Belgium, Luxembourg, Sweden, Ireland, the Netherlands, Estonia, and Finland, including chains like Delhaize, Carrefour, and Ikea. In northern Belgium alone, one Delhaize branch in Hoeilaart reported losing 100,000 euros worth of perishable produce.
