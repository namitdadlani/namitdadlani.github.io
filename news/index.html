Ontario will officially begin removing Toronto bike lanes on Tuesday

The Ontario government will begin removing protected bike lanes from a stretch of Toronto's Bloor Street West on Tuesday. Crews will dismantle pre-cast concrete curbs and pavement markings along a 1.2-kilometer section in Etobicoke to restore a motor vehicle lane in each direction. The move follows an Ontario Court of Appeal ruling upholding Premier Doug Ford's authority to remove 19 kilometers of bike infrastructure. Mayor Olivia Chow condemned the provincial action as an infringement on municipal governance. Work will take place mainly at night through November.
