Prop 45 in California could be hiding a loophole for data centers

California voters are considering Proposition 45, a ballot measure designed to fast-track environmental reviews for essential infrastructure, but critics warn it could let data center developers bypass public scrutiny. Supposedly aimed at speeding up housing and clean energy projects, the measure shortens environmental impact reports to 365 days and limits public comment to just 45 days. Environmental group Earthjustice warns developers might sneak in under the "broadband" category. Courts would also lose the power to completely stop non-compliant projects.

Here’s why parts of Ontario are not turning their clocks back this weekend

Parts of northwestern Ontario are abandoning the biannual clock change to permanently remain on daylight saving time starting November 1. While most of the province turns clocks back on Sunday, northwestern areas west of the 90th meridian are keeping their time fixed. Ontario passed a law in 2020 to end clock changes province-wide, but that legislation requires neighboring Quebec and New York to adopt permanent daylight time first. Until those trading partners act, the rest of Ontario remains bound to the traditional time switch.
