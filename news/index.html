Toronto police scrapping safety resource for vulnerable residents

The Toronto Police Service is decommissioning its Vulnerable Persons Registry on November 3. Launched in 2019, the voluntary database provided police with background information on neurodivergent and vulnerable residents. Police stated that newer technologies like RapidSOS provide more effective ways to share information with first responders, though advocates have raised concerns over lost peace of mind and potential costs for alternative third-party services.
