The backlash about data centers might really just be about electricity

Public backlash against massive AI data centers is primarily driven by their massive electricity demands rather than a cultural war over tech. In places like Virginia's Data Center Alley, an average facility consumes 54 megawatts—more than Denver International Airport. These huge power draws strain public grids, leading states to demand data centers pay for new transmission lines. To avoid local resistance, companies are turning to onsite natural gas turbines in rural areas or exploring overseas locations.

Claude Code is accidentally exposing secrets all over GitHub

Anthropic's Claude Code coding assistant is leaking sensitive credentials across public GitHub repositories through its local settings files. When users choose to automatically approve command prompts, Claude Code saves those commands—along with any embedded API tokens, passwords, or root logins—into a settings file. Because repository-level ignore settings are often missing, researchers found over 100,000 uploaded settings files, with roughly 1,100 containing active secrets. Popular scanning tools failed to flag over 200 of these leaks.

Newly elected California Congressman Scott Wiener is pushing for strict federal AI regulation after winning former Speaker Nancy Pelosi’s San Francisco seat. Russia's leading independent newspaper closes after eviction

Novaya Gazeta, Russia’s leading independent newspaper, has shut down after being evicted from its offices amidst pressure from government authorities. For 30 years, the publication investigated state corruption, human rights abuses, and official misuse of power across the country. Co-founded by Nobel Peace Prize winner Dmitry Muratov, the editorial team held their final meeting on Monday before packing up their belongings. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov dismissed the closure as part of a standard rotational process.

Honda Canada announces plans to retool Alliston, Ont., plant for hybrid production

Honda Canada is investing $867 million to retool its manufacturing plant in Alliston, Ontario, shifting focus toward hybrid-electric and gasoline vehicle production.
