An Israeli firm is sending spyware through online ads, researchers say

An Israeli ad tech firm named Discover-Tech worked with spyware companies like Candiru to infect at least 45 devices at Al Jazeera with spyware simply by showing users an online ad. Ricursive Intelligence is betting on AI-designed chips to help solve the compute crisis

Researchers use AI to design proteins that cut up other proteins

Researchers from Nobel-winner David Baker’s lab at the University of Washington used AI-based protein design tools to combine the catalytic cores of zinc and cysteine proteases with new protein structures. The resulting custom enzymes were successfully tuned to cut specific proteins in half, including those associated with neurological diseases. While the tools excelled at creating structures that bring necessary amino acids together, researchers noted they still lack the ability to specify exact catalytic activity.
