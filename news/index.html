AI chatbots are recommending fake retail websites, study finds

Popular AI chatbots are pointing online shoppers toward fake and deceptive retail websites, according to a study by IP protection firm Podqi. Researchers tested 151 brands on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, discovering that chatbots provided risky website links in up to 15 percent of their responses. Perplexity performed the worst with a 15 percent risk rate, while ChatGPT surfaced risky sites 8 percent of the time and Gemini 4 percent. ChatGPT offered warning labels on suspicious recommendations 24 percent of the time.
