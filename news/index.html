The Fridge-Hacking Spree Is an Internet-of-Things Wake-Up Call

A widespread wave of cyberattacks on internet-connected refrigerators has disrupted blood storage facilities and supermarkets across Europe and the United States. In France, cold rooms storing blood bags had their temperatures dangerously raised on November 2, though quick-acting staff saved the samples. Meanwhile, grocery stores in at least 11 European countries and six U.S. military bases were forced to discard spoiled food. Attackers exploited unpatched firmware and weak passwords in temperature controllers made by Danish firm Danfoss.

This Totally Free App Can Free Up Space on Your Mac

If your Mac hard drive is constantly full, a free, open-source app called Purge can easily clear out gigabytes of hidden clutter without ads or subscription fees. The tool safely targets temporary caches, leftover files from previously uninstalled applications, and massive screen saver files by sending them to the Trash. For users looking for even bigger space gains, Purge can completely remove Apple Intelligence offline AI models from macOS 27, unlocking over 10 gigabytes of storage.

Crusoe sells troubled Wyoming data center project to Google

Data center developer Crusoe has sold an unfinished data center project in southeastern Wyoming to Google. Google stepped in after expressing concerns about construction delays and costs under Crusoe's oversight, and now plans to invest $17 billion into building out the campus. Originally planned to scale to 10 gigawatts of power capacity, the project highlights the challenges independent developers face. Despite the sale, Crusoe remains valued at $30.9 billion and is currently developing a separate data center for Google near Amarillo, Texas.

No clear winner after tight New Zealand general election

New Zealand's general election ended without a clear winner on Saturday, leaving political leaders scrambling to negotiate a coalition government. Prime Minister Christopher Luxon's centre-right National Party led with 42 seats, falling short of the 61 required for a majority, while Chris Hipkins' Labour Party secured 36 seats.
