Astronomers Confirm Discovery of the Youngest Known Exoplanet Ever

Astronomers have confirmed the discovery of Elias 2-24b, the youngest exoplanet ever found. Located 450 light-years from Earth, this massive world orbits a star that is less than one million years old. That is a real head-scratcher for scientists, as current core accretion models say a giant planet should take five million years to form. The research team confirmed its existence by comparing orbital positions recorded in 2018 and 2020 at Hawaii's W.M. Keck Observatory.

Nobel Prize awarded for revealing inner workings of the brain

Scientists Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel have won this year's Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for developing optogenetics. This revolutionary method uses a special protein from algae to turn specific brain pathways on and off using light. The discovery allows researchers to study memories, feelings, and behaviors, and is even being tested to help blind patients regain their sight. The three winners will share a prize of 12 million Swedish kronor.
