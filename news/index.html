Meta’s new terms may allow it to train AI on news sites for free

Meta's updated commercial terms taking effect October 30 could allow the company to train its AI models on news websites without paying licensing fees. The new policy grants Meta permission to crawl and index any website associated with business accounts using Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or Messenger. Brazil sees record fall in Amazon deforestation as key election looms

Deforestation in Brazil's Amazon rainforest plummeted by a record 41.8% in the year through July 2026.
