Goodfire says its new ‘inside-out’ monitors catch rogue AI agents at a fraction of the cost

AI safety startup Goodfire has launched a cheaper way to stop rogue AI agents by monitoring their internal calculations as they run. Instead of paying a second AI to reread everything a model writes, Goodfire's system uses small detectors called "probes" to analyze internal signals. In tests on the Kimi K3 model, monitoring 1,500 sessions cost just $51, compared to $10,000 using a top-tier traditional AI checker. The probes successfully caught 94% of malicious hacking sessions while adding less than 2% to response times.

Toronto-born poet Anne Carson awarded Nobel Prize in Literature

Toronto-born poet and essayist Anne Carson has been awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature. The 76-year-old author, who was in Iceland "listening to the wind" when she received the news, was recognized by the Swedish Academy for her inventive writing that plays with classical traditions. Carson is the third Canadian-born writer to win the prize, following Alice Munro and Saul Bellow. She was diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease two years ago. The award comes with a cash prize of 12 million Swedish crowns, or $1.7 million Canadian.
