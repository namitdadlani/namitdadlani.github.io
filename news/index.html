The iPhone Duo Is Getting a Limited Version of Face ID

Apple is bringing a limited form of facial recognition to its expensive foldable iPhone Duo via the new iOS 27.3 software update. Because the device lacks the hardware sensors required for full Face ID, Apple adapted person-recognition technology from its HomeView smart display. The feature unlocks the front screen without tapping, but it cannot authorize payments or open banking apps. Users must also have unlocked the phone using Touch ID or a passcode within the previous 45 minutes.

Instagram Is Bringing Amazon Affiliate Links Directly to Reels and Posts

Instagram is letting creators add Amazon affiliate links directly to their Reels and feed posts, cutting down on the need for link-in-bio detours. US-based adult creators with professional accounts and over 1,000 followers can attach up to 30 Amazon links per video or 20 per post. US moves to cut China’s remote access to AI chips

The US Commerce Department is closing a regulatory loophole to stop rivals like China, Russia, and Iran from remotely accessing restricted American AI chips. Foreign entities previously bypassed export bans by renting server capacity from data centers in countries like Singapore, Malaysia, and Brazil. Under the new rules, data centers in those nations facing higher diversion risks must apply for licenses and submit monthly transaction reports. The policy change officially takes effect 90 days after its publication.

FBI says Artex AI open-source hacking tool used to breach networks in ‘under three hours’

The FBI is warning that hackers are using an open-source AI penetration tool called Artex to compromise corporate networks in under three hours. By scanning for basic security flaws and stolen passwords, the automated software allows attackers to breach systems rapidly. Recent Artex attacks compromised South Korean financial firms, exposing data from 68,000 people, and breached IT provider ConnectWise, affecting over 2,250 customer accounts. Although the developer shut down open access, older versions of Artex remain widely available.

Five EU countries announce migrant deportation centre in Rwanda

Provincial bill could pave the way for more trash incineration to make energy

The Ontario government has introduced legislation to expand waste-to-energy incineration facilities as the province faces a looming landfill crisis.
