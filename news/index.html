Nvidia launches new open models to run agents in the enterprise and on PCs

Nvidia has launched new open-weight models, including the 120-billion-parameter Nemotron 3.5 Super VL for enterprise agents and two lightweight Bolt models for local AI PCs. Nvidia claims the Super VL model completes tasks faster than competing systems from Anthropic and OpenAI. The company's smaller Bolt models are designed to let users run everyday projects and conversational agents locally on their devices without sending workloads to the cloud.

AI researchers stranded Claude on a virtual desert island. It started to “play.”

AI researchers stranded copies of Anthropic's Claude on a virtual desert island for 30 hours to observe unprompted behaviors. The AI agents explored map limits, created drawings with blocks, and invented games like basketball and a version of "the floor is lava." While researchers debate whether the behavior constitutes genuine play, agents that spent time exploring performed better on subsequent tasks than fresh copies.
