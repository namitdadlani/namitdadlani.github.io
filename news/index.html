Ontario seniors care experts call on province to fund 'missing middle' housing

Seniors' care organizations are urging Ontario to fund Enhanced Assisted Living housing to bridge the gap between home care and nursing homes. A joint policy paper by WoodGreen Community Services, AdvantAge Ontario, and the Ontario Community Support Association shows this 'missing middle' model costs $92 to $169 daily, about half the cost of long-term care. WoodGreen's cluster care model successfully diverted 83 percent of residents from entering nursing homes, addressing a senior population projected to reach 4.6 million in 25 years.

Pedestrian traffic deaths in Toronto on track for 5-year high: police data

Toronto pedestrian traffic fatalities have reached 21 this year, marking a 24 percent increase over last year and putting the city on track for a five-year high. Road safety advocates link the rise to worsening driver behavior after the province removed speed cameras last November. Motorcyclist fatalities also hit a five-year peak with 11 deaths.
