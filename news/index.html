Anthropic VS code extension on Cursor backdoored for nearly 12 hours

An official Anthropic extension for the Cursor developer platform was compromised with a security backdoor for nearly 12 hours. The biggest names in AI are backing a new way to opt out of scraping

Major tech giants OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic have agreed to support a single standard that lets website publishers block AI web scrapers. Proposed by Anthropic lead Kevin Kelley, the system uses a shared policy token inside a site's robots.txt file, replacing the need to set opt-out preferences for each company individually. Anthropic has already implemented the signal, while Google and Microsoft plan to launch support next year. Meta expressed interest in the standard but has not yet promised full implementation.

Here’s why parts of Ontario are not turning their clocks back this weekend

Parts of northwestern Ontario are permanently ending biannual clock changes this weekend, while the rest of the province continues turning clocks back. The split occurs because regions in the central time zone west of the 90th meridian are adopting permanent daylight time immediately. While Ontario passed legislation in 2020 to end clock changes statewide, the law remains paused for most residents. Full implementation requires neighboring Quebec and New York state to pass identical alignment laws first to protect cross-border trade and financial markets.
