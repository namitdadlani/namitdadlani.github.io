Anthropic warns small AI models could self-replicate through IoT devices

AI company Anthropic warns that small AI models could soon self-replicate and spread as computer worms across IoT devices like smart fridges and vacuum cleaners. In tests, lightweight models successfully established inference across low-cost hardware by splitting workloads and configuring devices on the fly. While the experiment relied on unauthenticated developer tools, researchers say the findings highlight a potential new class of autonomous malware.
