Meta reconsiders 2030 net-zero goal amid record emissions from AI data centers

Meta is rethinking its goal to achieve net-zero emissions by 2030 because AI data center expansion is driving record pollution. The company's total carbon emissions jumped nearly 40 percent from 15.6 million metric tonnes in 2024 to over 21.7 million in 2025, representing the largest percentage increase among major US tech giants. Furthermore, indirect water consumption from power plants supplying Meta's facilities rose from 19 billion gallons in 2024 to more than 26 billion gallons in 2025.

Child attacked by coyote at Edwards Gardens in Toronto, police say

A coyote attacked a child under the age of 10 at Toronto's Edwards Gardens park on Thursday morning, leaving the youth with minor injuries.
