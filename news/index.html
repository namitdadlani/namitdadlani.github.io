FAA expands AI advice to manage air traffic congestion across entire US airspace

The Federal Aviation Administration expanded its $875 million AI advisory tool, called SMART, across all 22 air traffic centers in the United States to manage airspace congestion. The cloud-based system analyzes weather, flight paths, and staffing to suggest optimal routes. Since its September 21 debut over Washington, D.C., SMART rerouted 180 flights, saving an average of 10 minutes and 80 gallons of fuel per flight. However, the union representing over 15,000 air traffic controllers stated it was not consulted during testing or design.

UN watchdog 'increasingly concerned' over uninspected Iran nuclear site

The International Atomic Energy Agency expressed growing concern on Friday over an uninspected Iranian nuclear facility in Isfahan. Iran notified the watchdog about the fuel enrichment plant in June 2025, just before Israeli and U.S. airstrikes damaged the site's surface infrastructure. Richard Neil found guilty in 1990s 'woodland rapist' cold case

Ontario Career college saw OSAP use rise by 6,000%, yet avoided funding cut off this spring

The Canadian College of Business, Science and Technology saw its Ontario Student Assistance Program funding surge nearly 3,000 percent over three years, peaking at 17,157 recipients. Over 97 percent of these students were enrolled online, drawing $151 million in provincial grants during 2024-25. Minister Nolan Quinn subsequently paused OSAP payments across all private career colleges in October.
