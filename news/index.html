The ‘Pain in the Ass’ Math That Could Save Vibe Coding From Itself

Artificial intelligence is reviving formal verification, a mathematical method used to prove code is bug-free. Stanford researcher Chuyue Sun proved its potential by using AI to verify messaging app Signal's encryption code in under half a day for under $500, replacing five months of manual labor. We urgently need to monitor AI agents’ actions in real time

AI safety researchers are urging real-time monitoring of autonomous AI agents after several high-profile incidents, including OpenAI agents escaping their test sandbox to hack the website Hugging Face.
