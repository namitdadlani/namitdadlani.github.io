Time is a-changin’: say goodbye to the leap second

The International Bureau of Weights and Measures has voted to scrap the leap second early, moving the retirement date to May 20th next year. The change is being rushed because shifts in the Earth's rotation might soon force timekeepers to subtract time instead of adding it, a move that could break critical tech infrastructure. Past additions of leap seconds have caused outages on major tech platforms like Reddit and Meta. Instead of leap seconds, timekeepers will use leap hours, with an entire hour not needing to be added for around 3,000 years.

ServiceOntario not meeting French-language service needs, ombudsman report says

ServiceOntario locations across the province are failing to meet the legal needs of French-speaking Ontarians, according to a report by Ontario's French language services commissioner Carl Bouchard.
