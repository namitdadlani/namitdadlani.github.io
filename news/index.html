‘The show is starting soon. Please turn on your headphones.’

The off-Broadway one-man show Love Me uses Bluetooth Auracast technology to let audiences experience hearing loss firsthand through wireless headphones. Created by comedian Drew Michael and director Prashanth Venkataramanujam, the play combines muffled theater speakers with a clear headphone feed of Michael's inner monologue. During the climax, audience members take off their headphones as Michael confronts his denial and embraces his Deaf identity. The production also offers specialized Auracast feeds for hearing aids and iPads with captions.

8 Best Travel Adapters (2026): 20+ Models Tested

Selecting the right travel adapter depends on your destination and device voltage needs. Universal adapters cover most of the world's 15 plug types, though countries like India, Israel, and South Africa require specific single-plug adapters. Most modern electronics support variable voltages from 100 to 220 volts, making heavy voltage converters unnecessary. When buying, ensure the adapter supplies enough wattage for your devices, such as 100 watts for laptops or 30 watts for smartphones.

Signal restricts mobile screenshots as it confronts ‘existential threat’ of AI surveillance

Encrypted messaging app Signal is restricting screenshot capabilities on Android and iOS devices to protect user privacy from AI surveillance. Signal President Meredith Whittaker called operating system AI agents an existential threat because they scan screen content and store sensitive data on external servers beyond Signal's control. Signal previously blocked screenshots on Windows by default. While users can manually turn off the restriction, Whittaker warned that disabling it voids the app's privacy guarantees.
